Notes on quantum information, mathematics, and research.

  1. Constructing Qubit Channels with Arbitrary Fixed Points

    Sep 2026

    Quantum Channels

    Motivated by the Banach fixed-point theorem, we construct a single-qubit channel, a rotated convex combination of the depolarizing and amplitude damping channels, whose repeated application drives any initial state to any chosen target state. Includes an interactive Bloch-sphere explorer of the trajectory.

  2. Introduction to Cycle Benchmarking for Quantum Error Diagnostics

    Dec 2025

    Error Characterization

    A scalable, SPAM-robust protocol for estimating the process fidelity of a fixed cycle of parallel gates. We introduce the CB protocol, explain how randomized compiling tailors noise into Pauli channels, and analyze when the gate-independence assumption can be relaxed.

  3. Self-Consistent Gauges for Cycle Benchmarking under Gate-Dependent Noise

    Nov 2025

    Error Characterization

    When the randomizing gates of cycle benchmarking have gate-dependent errors, its estimates fall far below their targets. Comparing against a gauge-rotated target, built from the leading Kraus operator of an averaged channel, closes much of the gap. We also show why randomized compiling still tailors gate-dependent easy-gate noise into Pauli channels.

  4. Superoperator Representations and the Characterization of Completely Positive Maps

    Aug 2024

    Quantum Channels

    Six ways to write a quantum channel (Kraus operators, Stinespring dilations, Liouville and Pauli transfer matrices, Choi matrices and χ-matrices), how to convert between them, and how complete positivity and trace preservation look in each. Based on my AMATH 900 project at the University of Waterloo.
