I am a Master's student at the University of Waterloo and a researcher at the Institute for Quantum Computing, under the supervision of Graeme Smith. In Fall 2027, I'll join the Université de Sherbrooke to pursue a PhD in mathematics with Maxence Mayrand and Baptiste Royer as my supervisors.

My research focuses on quantum error correction under non-Markovian noise. Using the process tensor framework, I study how QEC codes interact with temporally correlated errors and whether error correction can effectively suppress or Markovianize the noise process. More broadly, I'm interested in fault-tolerant quantum computing, open quantum systems, and quantum error mitigation and characterization.
